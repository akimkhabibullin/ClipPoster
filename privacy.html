Privacy Policy

clipposter is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload their own videos to their own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts.

It requests permission to upload videos and read basic profile info (account name) so the owner can see which account is connected. Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. No data is sold, shared or sent to any server other than the platform the video is being posted to.

You can revoke access at any time from your Google, TikTok or Instagram account settings.
