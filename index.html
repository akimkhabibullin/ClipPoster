Post once. Everywhere.

A personal publishing tool for short-form video.

clipposter uploads my own finished short-form clips from my computer to the YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts I own, in one step, using each platform's official API.

What it does

  • Uploads videos with a title, caption and tags to accounts I've connected
  • Only uploads. It doesn't read, collect or analyze anyone's data
  • Runs only on my own computer and isn't offered to other users

clipposter is an independent project and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube, Google, TikTok or Meta.
